i am so tired writing this.

worked all day yesterday. will go out today with the friend i made 2 days ago. i told him i would love to trek. man made me walk a lot.

walked around the campus and to a nearby shrine.

he had discovered an abandoned shrine while walking with his friends a few days ago. went back to the falls. completely walked the path this time.

the name of the waterfall is Junigataki which literally means "twelve waterfalls". you guessed it right, because it has 12 waterfalls. luckily there were no snakes or bears.

this time walked around tsurugi. the place is beautiful. it was hot now though. we had local sweets. japanese like gelatine ?

we were tired from all the walking so we went inside a sitting place. i think its a literal resting place for travellers.

saw a buddhist shrine. apparently there are a lot of shrines. went to another shrine. was fortunate enough to see a ritual there. it was a shinto temple. there are buddhist, hindu and shinto temples here. the path to the highlight shrine of the trip was something out of a picture. they would light up the lamps on the edges during night.

the living standards of people here are so good. we were walking around a small town btw.

we walked around. rode a cable car. visited a unique restaurant (best food). and a wood workshop. bought something from each place for friends and family.

i had an urgent meeting to attend. i went inside a cafe. my friend wanted to buy something so he went to a mall. it was a bad idea. it was crowded and noisy.

i feel like the cafes and restaurants have a personality. walked around the city a little more and went back home.

my legs were sore from all this travelling. we missed the bus again :sobbing:.
